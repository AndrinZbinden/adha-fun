How it works

1
Pick an adha

Ten presets: buy and burn, holder rewards, jackpot, treasury. Or write the split in a sentence and read the compiled legs before you commit.

2
Launch on pump.fun

Name, ticker, image, optional dev buy. The policy is written on chain first, then the mint goes out. Both are signed in your own wallet.

3
Run the adha

Claim your creator fees and the split executes against what actually landed. Every leg is a transaction anyone can follow.

Why an adha is worth anything

Written before the first trade

The split goes on chain in its own transaction, confirmed before the mint. It is readable by anyone deciding whether to buy.

Adha never holds the money

There is no custody and no treasury of ours. Every buy, burn and payout is a transaction your wallet signs.

Each adha states its limits

A burn is not a price floor. A weighted jackpot favours whales. Every adha says so on its own page, in plain language.
